🦸‍♂️ Marvel vs. DC – A Data-Driven Comparison
This repository is dedicated to an in-depth analysis of Marvel and DC, the two biggest superhero franchises in the world. By leveraging data science, 
machine learning, and visualization techniques, we aim to explore the similarities, differences, and trends in their movies, comics, characters, and box office performance.

📌 Features
🎬 Movies & Box Office Analysis
Total Box Office Revenue: Comparing Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) and DC Extended Universe (DCEU) performance.
IMDb & Rotten Tomatoes Ratings: Analyzing audience and critic scores over time.
Budget vs. Revenue Analysis: Understanding profitability trends.
Genre & Tone Comparison: Examining action, comedy, and dark/gritty storytelling styles.


📚 Comics & Character Analysis
Number of Comics Published: Which franchise has more titles and storylines?
Superhero Power Comparisons: Strength, intelligence, durability, speed, and abilities.
Character Popularity Trends: Based on social media mentions, Google Trends, and fan votes.
DC vs. Marvel Villains: Ranking the most powerful and feared villains.
🏆 Superhero Battles & Fan Debates
Head-to-Head Superhero Matchups: Who would win based on abilities and feats?
Crossover Events & Storyline Comparisons: Exploring iconic Marvel vs. DC collaborations.
Fandom Engagement: Which universe has the most passionate fanbase online?
